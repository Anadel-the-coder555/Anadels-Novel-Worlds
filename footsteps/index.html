Grandmother’s Footsteps

Hold Space (or the Sneak button) and everyone creeps forward. Let go and they freeze. While “it” chants, you’re safe. When they spin round, anyone still moving goes back to the start.

Your friends don’t all stop at once. Lila-Bella always skips one step too many, and the princes wobble if they freeze with one foot in the air. Stop a little early!
